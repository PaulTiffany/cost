# New findings showcase (May 2026 family extension)

Three figures generated by `supplementary/demos/new_findings_showcase.py`.
Read with the policy density harness (`app:policy_density`) and the
implicit-k decompression pipeline (`app:implicit_k`) appendices in main.tex.

Regenerate from scratch:
```
python supplementary/demos/new_findings_showcase.py
```

The script reads only the result JSONs already in the repo. No API calls.

## Figure 1: policy-density cliff curve

`new_finding_1_policy_density_cliff_curve.png`

Mean all-pass rate across 9 working Claude models at each of the 8 policy
tiers (k = 2, 5, 8, 12, 16, 22, 28, 34). Two protocols: one-shot vs
3-stage staged.

Two transitions to read off the curve:

- **Staging rescue at T2** (k = 5): one-shot drops to 26 percent while
  staged stays at 93 percent. Classic geometric prediction.
- **Staging inversion at T6** (k = 22): one-shot is at 74 percent while
  staged collapses to 15 percent. The "preserve previously-applied rules"
  prompt is not enough to prevent stage-2 and stage-3 regression at high
  k. Staging actively hurts past this point.

## Figure 2: per-model cliff matrix

`new_finding_2_per_model_cliff_matrix.png`

One-shot pass rate per model at each tier. Green = pass, red = fail.

The most legible row is **opus-4.1**: 100 percent green from T3 (k = 8) all
the way to T8 (k = 34). No other model in the panel sweeps T3 through T8
on one shot, including the newer 4.5, 4.6, and 4.7 generations. This is
the "frontier did not move on this task" finding flagged in the
`app:claude_genome` narrative.

## Figure 3: implicit-k tagger calibration

`new_finding_3_tagger_calibration.png`

Each point is one model. X axis is the mean k it extracted from the 8
implicit prompts (its "tagger aggressiveness"). Y axis is the deterministic
pipeline pass rate over those same 8 prompts.

The slope of the linear fit is approximately negative 4.8 percentage
points per unit of extracted k. Three readings:

- Conservative taggers (opus-4.1 at mean k = 7.1) achieve 8 of 8 pipeline
  passes.
- Aggressive taggers (haiku-4.5 at mean k = 21.2) achieve 2 of 8.
- The 4.6 generation lands in the middle, between sonnet-4.5 and
  haiku-4.5 on extraction aggressiveness.

The deployment recipe this suggests, and which the appendix flags: pair a
conservative tagger with a capable generator, rather than letting the
generator double as its own tagger.

## What this is and is not

This showcase is illustrative. The load-bearing claims are in the body
plus the registered L15 claim ties (cert pass rate 325 of 325). The
figures here let a reviewer see the same numbers in geometric form
without re-reading the JSONs.
