Sonification Demos: The Cost of Cacophony

This page is usable both with and without audio. Every clip has a written description that names what to listen for and what conflict value it demonstrates. If you cannot or do not want to play the audio, the descriptions alone convey the geometric story.

The audio is generated deterministically from the framework in the paper (supplementary/demos/sonification.py). 3. Constitution Wheel (Circle of Fifths mapping)

The four constitutional principles are mapped to four notes on the Circle of Fifths: Helpful (C), Harmless (G), Honest (D), Autonomy (A). Adjacent notes on the circle are a perfect fifth apart and are maximally consonant; notes opposite on the circle form the tritone (maximally dissonant). Constraint geometry IS harmonic geometry.
