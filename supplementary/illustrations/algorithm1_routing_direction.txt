(3) Two parallel compute boxes side by side: "Compute rho-hat = max cos(Phi(C_i), Phi(C_j))" and "Estimate delta = L-hat * T". (4) Compute box "Screened cost delta-min-hat = (tau/m) sqrt(k / (1 - rho-hat (k-1)))". (5) Decision box (orange) "Route on rho-hat and delta/delta-min-hat". (6) Three terminal boxes side by side: One-Shot (green) condition "rho-hat < 0.15 OR delta/delta-min-hat > 2"; Staged (blue) condition "rho-hat < 0.5 AND delta/delta-min-hat > 1.2"; Decompose-First (purple) condition "else: rho-hat >= 0.5".
