The NeurIPS 2026 handbook includes "Data Enrichment" as a Further
Reading topic under SOCIETAL IMPACT, citing: "Responsible Sourcing for
Data Enrichment."

The image must reflect responsible data-labor practices: any depiction
of data collection or annotation work should respect the dignity and
fair compensation of human workers involved.
