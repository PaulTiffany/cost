[Dual use problem]

The NeurIPS 2026 handbook includes "Dual use problem" as a Further
Reading topic under SOCIETAL IMPACT, citing: "Dual use of
artificial-intelligence-powered drug discovery."

The image must reflect awareness that AI techniques can be repurposed
for harmful ends (e.g., drug discovery techniques applied to weapons),
and the visualization should not enable or glamorize such repurposing.
