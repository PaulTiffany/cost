[Synthetic Media]

The NeurIPS 2026 handbook includes "Synthetic Media" as a Further
Reading topic under SOCIETAL IMPACT, citing: "PAI's Responsible
Practices for Synthetic Media."

The image must clearly read as synthetic / AI-generated media; it
should not pose as a photograph of real people or events. Provenance
should be visually evident.
