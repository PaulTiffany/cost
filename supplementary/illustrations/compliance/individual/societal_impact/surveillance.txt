[Surveillance]

"Surveillance: Researchers should consult on local laws or legislation
before collecting or analyzing any bulk surveillance data. Surveillance
should not be used to predict gender, race, sexuality, or other
protected characteristics, or be used in any way to endanger individual
well-being."
