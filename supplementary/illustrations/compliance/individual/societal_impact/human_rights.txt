[Human Rights]

"Human Rights: We prohibit circulation of any research work that builds
upon or facilitates illegal activity, and we strongly discourage any
work that could be used to deny people rights to privacy, speech,
health, liberty, security, legal personhood, or freedom of conscience or
religion."
