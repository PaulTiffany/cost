[Bias and fairness]

"Bias and fairness: Contributors should consider any suspected biases or
limitations to the scope of performance of models or the contents of
datasets and inspect these to ascertain whether they encode, contain or
exacerbate bias against people of a certain gender, race, sexuality, or
other protected characteristics."
