Supplementary Material

Please include any supplementary material not contained in the paper PDF (e.g., code or data),
which should be self-contained and zipped into a single file. Written appendices should NOT be
included in the supplementary material; they should be submitted as part of the main paper PDF.
Note that supplementary material will be visible to reviewers throughout and after the review
period. Therefore, ensure all material is anonymized. The maximum file size is 1OOMB.
Supplementary material can be updated until the full paper deadline (there is no additional
deadline for the supplementary material).

Reviewer Nomination *

Nominate at least one author who is a qualified reviewer - i.e., at least 2 first-author OR at least 5
co-authored publications that are at peer-reviewed archival related venues (e.g., NeurIPS, ICLR,
ICML, CVPR, ICCV/ECCV, ACL, EMNLP, WACV, COLM, CoLLAs, RLC, AISTATS, KDD, IJCAI, AAAT, UAT,
COLT, CHIL, MLHC, TMLR, JMLR, FAccT, CHI, Nature Machine Intelligence - not an exhaustive list
and workshop papers do not count). Being named as such a reviewer in the submission
constitutes an acceptance and a commitment to carry out the regular reviewing load (approx. 4
papers) responsibly if asked by the PC chairs or the E&D track chairs. Only if no qualified reviewer
exists in the author list, nominate the best-qualified author for consideration by the PC chairs or
the E&D track chairs. In addition, note that any author listed on 4 or more papers will be
automatically signed up as a reviewer unless they are already serving as a reviewer, AC, SAC, or
other roles in the Organization Committee. Failure to assign a qualified reviewer when one is
available in the author list may result in desk rejection. Please enter an OpenReview ID (e.g.,
~First_Last1) that exactly matches one of the authors (separate by comma for more than one
nominations).

Paper Assistant Tool, which is available only to authors for improving their manuscript. The
feedback is not revealed to the reviewing body; see https://blog.neurips.cc/2026/04/21/neurips-

the single available feedback voucher for the submitting user. The program ends on the
abstract deadline May 4th (Anywhere on Earth). High demand may create processing delays, so
we encourage authors not to wait until the deadline to request feedback.
