Call For Papers 2026

The Fortieth Annual Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems
(NeurlPS 2026) is an interdisciplinary conference that brings together
researchers in deep learning, generative Al, core machine learning, neuroscience,
statistics, optimization, computer vision, natural language processing, life
sciences, natural sciences, social sciences, and other adjacent fields. We invite
submissions presenting new and original research on topics including but not
limited to the following:

e Computer vision

e Language and multimodal language models

e Robotics, embodied systems, and engineering

e Al/ML for physical sciences

e Al/ML for health and biotechnology

e Al/ML for sustainability

e AI/ML for social sciences

e AI/ML for creatives

e Neuroscience and cognitive science

e Socio-technical aspects of Al

e Human interaction in Al systems

e Decision-making, reinforcement learning, and control

e Generalization and multi-task learning

e Optimization

e Probabilistic methods

e Al and network science

e Data-centric aspects of Al

e SysML Infrastructure

e Theory

¢ Deep learning

e General machine learning: core contributions in supervised and unsupervised
methods

Machine learning is a rapidly evolving field, and so we welcome interdisciplinary
submissions that do not fit neatly into existing categories. We also encourage in-
depth analysis of existing methods that provide new insights in terms of their
limitations or behavior beyond the scope of the original work.

Key dates:

e Abstract submission deadline: May 4, 2026 (AOE). All authors must have an

e Full paper submission deadline, including all supplementary materials: May

e Author notification: September 24, 2026 AoE
