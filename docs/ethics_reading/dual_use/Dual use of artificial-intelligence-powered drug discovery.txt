Dual use of artificial-intelligence-powered drug discovery

Fabio Urbina, Filippa Lentzos, Cédric Invernizzi & Sean Ekins MJ

Nature Machine Intelligence 4, 189-191 (2022)

127k Accesses | 326 Citations | 3608 Altmetric | Metrics

An international security conference explored how artificial intelligence (AI)
technologies for drug discovery could be misused for de novo design of

biochemical weapons. A thought experiment evolved into a computational proof.
